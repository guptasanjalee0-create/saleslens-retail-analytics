# Findings and business recommendations

> Replace this template with findings generated from the **full official UCI dataset**. Do not use the synthetic demo numbers in a public report as real business findings.

## Key findings

1. **Revenue trend:** Describe the highest and lowest revenue months, with exact values from your analysis.
2. **Product performance:** Identify the top products by revenue and distinguish revenue from quantity sold.
3. **Geography:** Describe the highest-revenue countries and note that the dataset is from one UK-based retailer.
4. **Customer patterns:** Describe RFM segment sizes and the proportion of customers with missing IDs.
5. **Data quality:** Report duplicate rows, cancellations, invalid quantities/prices and missing customer IDs.

## Recommendations

Write two or three evidence-based recommendations. Explain the analysis behind each recommendation and avoid claims the data cannot support.
